About this model
Tehran (تهران) sits on the southern slope of the Alborz range, climbing from about 1,050 m in the south to over 1,700 m in the north — a 600 m tilt you can feel while walking. Landmarks are placed at their true relative bearings from real coordinates, with horizontal distances compressed ≈2.2× so the whole city is walkable. Vertical scale is true. Street fabric, traffic and crowds are generated procedurally.
